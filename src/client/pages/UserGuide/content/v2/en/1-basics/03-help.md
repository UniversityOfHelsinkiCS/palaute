# Problems and support

Solutions to the most common problems. If yours isn't solved, contact [support](#support).

## My course has no survey {#no-survey}

- Surveys are created for teaching only, not e.g. for exams or theses. See [Which courses get a survey](/guide/schedule#which-courses).
- If the course page says [[feedbackTargetView:feedbackDisabled]], the degree programme has [switched off feedback](/guide/programmes#disable-feedback) for the course unit.
- If a course that should get a survey is missing from [[navBar:myCourses]] altogether, check your role on it in Sisu. See [If your role is wrong](/guide/roles#wrong-role).

## I can't edit the survey {#cannot-edit}

- The questions are locked when the survey opens, so that all students answer the same questions. After that, only the closing date can be changed; see [Changing the dates](/guide/schedule#change-dates).
- The course's responsible teachers and administrative persons can edit the survey, and the programme's admins its questions. With the teacher role, you see it only as a preview. See [Roles on a course](/guide/roles#course-roles).
- The university level and programme questions can't be edited in a course survey. See [Questions](/guide/questions).

## The survey has already closed {#closed}

A closed course survey's dates can't be changed in Norppa. If it must be reopened, contact [support](#support).

You can reopen interim feedback or a programme survey yourself by moving its end date. See [Interim feedback](/guide/continuous-and-interim-feedback#interim-feedback) and [Editing a programme survey](/guide/programme-surveys#edit).

## Students haven't got the survey {#students-not-reached}

- Students get an email about a course survey on its opening day, but none about interim feedback or programme surveys. See [Emails from Norppa](/guide/schedule#emails).
- You can reach students with a [reminder](/guide/collecting-answers#send-reminder) or by sharing [the survey's link](/guide/collecting-answers#share-link).
- Only students whose enrolment in Sisu is confirmed can answer. A new enrolment usually shows in Norppa within a couple of hours; see [Updates from Sisu](/guide/schedule#sisu-updates).

## I get emails about a course I'm not responsible for {#unexpected-emails}

Norppa sends the responsible teachers' emails to everyone who is the course's responsible teacher, contact person or administrative person in Sisu. The role can only be fixed in Sisu; see [If your role is wrong](/guide/roles#wrong-role).

## I can't see the answers {#cannot-see-answers}

- With the teacher role or read rights to a degree programme, you see only the answers to public questions. See [Who sees which answers](/guide/results#public-answers).
- If fewer than 5 students are enrolled, only the responsible teachers and administrative persons see the answers. See [Courses with fewer than 5 students](/guide/results#small-courses).
- New answers appear when you reload the page.

## A course is missing from the course summary {#missing-from-summary}

The course summary's figures are recalculated every night, and it includes only course surveys that have opened. Other reasons are listed under [Why a course is missing](/guide/course-summary#missing-courses).

## An answer must be removed {#remove-answer}

Answers can't be deleted in Norppa. You can [hide](/guide/results#hide-answers) an open answer, so that only the responsible teachers and programme admins still see it. If an answer must be removed entirely, contact [support](#support).

## Support {#support}

Support is at [norppa@helsinki.fi](mailto:norppa@helsinki.fi). Most things can be done in Norppa or fixed in Sisu, but contact support for:

- reopening a closed course survey, or changing its dates;
- a survey for a course type that doesn't get one automatically, such as independent work, an essay, training or a master's thesis (not for exams);
- a course, teacher or student that's still missing from Norppa a few days after the data in Sisu was fixed (Sisu changes normally show the next morning; see [Sisu updates](/guide/schedule#sisu-updates));
- an error that doesn't go away.

To help support help you, include a link to the survey's page or the course code, what you tried and what happened.

Ideas and feedback about Norppa itself go to its developers through [[navBar:feedback]] in the top menu.

## Data protection {#data-protection}

Norppa is a University of Helsinki service. The [[feedbackView:dataProtectionNotice]] link at the bottom of every Norppa page leads to the university's data protection information, which is collected on the university's [data protection page](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/data-protection).

What this means for the feedback:

- Students log in to Norppa, so it knows who may answer which survey. Teachers and programme staff still can't see who gave which answers. Open answers are listed in alphabetical order, each question's answers are shuffled separately in the download, and downloaded answers contain no student information.
- Students may write personal data, their own or someone else's, in open answers. Check the answers before making an open question public, and [hide](/guide/results#hide-answers) any that shouldn't be seen.
- In the survey, students can read how their answers are used under the link [[feedbackView:feedbackInfoLink]]

If you have questions about how Norppa handles data, contact [norppa@helsinki.fi](mailto:norppa@helsinki.fi).
