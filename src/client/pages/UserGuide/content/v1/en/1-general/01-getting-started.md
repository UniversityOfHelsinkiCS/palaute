# Getting started

Norppa is the course feedback system of the University of Helsinki. Students answer feedback surveys. Teachers edit the surveys, read the answers and reply to them with counter feedback. Degree programmes and faculties follow the feedback of their courses.

Course, teacher and student data comes from Sisu. All feedback is anonymous: teachers and programme staff can't see which student gave which answers.

## How a survey works {#survey-lifecycle}

Every survey is upcoming, open or closed, and only its dates decide which. There's no publish or send step:

1. Before it opens, the survey's responsible teachers can change its questions and its dates.
2. It opens by itself at 00:00 on its opening date. From then on, the questions are locked, and only the closing date can still be changed. Students can answer, and change or delete their answers, until it closes.
3. It closes by itself at 23:59 on its closing date (Finnish time).
4. After it has closed, the answers stay available. For 12 months, the responsible teachers can write [counter feedback](/guide/counter-feedback) to the students.

## Four kinds of feedback {#survey-types}

- _Course survey_: one for each course realisation, created automatically from Sisu. It has the university level questions and possibly the degree programme's own questions, to which the teachers add their own. Most of this guide is about course surveys. See [Course surveys](/guide/course-surveys).
- _Continuous feedback_: free-form feedback students can write about a course at any time before its course survey opens. It's off until a responsible teacher switches it on. See [Continuous feedback](/guide/continuous-and-interim-feedback#continuous-feedback).
- _Interim feedback_: an extra survey a responsible teacher creates for their course, e.g. in the middle of it. See [Interim feedback](/guide/continuous-and-interim-feedback#interim-feedback).
- _Programme survey_: a survey a degree programme or faculty creates for chosen students, e.g. semester feedback. See [Programme surveys](/guide/programme-surveys).

Continuous feedback has no questions, and the responsible teachers reply to each piece of it separately. Editing the questions, sharing the survey, reading the answers and counter feedback work the same way in the other three.

## Finding your way {#navigation}

The menu at the top shows only the items that concern you:

- [[navBar:myProgrammes]] or [[navBar:myOrganisations]]: the degree programmes and faculties you have rights to. The item is called [[navBar:myProgrammes]] if all of them are bachelor's or master's programmes.
- [[navBar:myCourses]]: the surveys of your courses, and programme surveys you're responsible for.
- [[navBar:courseSummary]]: feedback statistics of your courses, and of the degree programmes and faculties you have rights to.
- [[navBar:myFeedbacks]]: surveys you can answer yourself as a student.
- [[navBar:browseCourses]]: a degree programme's courses by start date, with links to their pages in the Studies Service.
- [[navBar:feedback]]: feedback about Norppa itself to its developers.
- Your name, top right: [[navBar:userInformation]] and the language of the user interface.

To open a survey, go to [[navBar:myCourses]] and choose the course. The survey's page shows the course's dates, its teachers and the tabs you can use. The tabs depend on your [role](/guide/roles) and on whether the survey is upcoming, open or closed.

## Support {#support}

Support is at [norppa@helsinki.fi](mailto:norppa@helsinki.fi). Most things can be done in Norppa or fixed in Sisu, but contact support for:

- reopening a closed course survey, or changing its dates;
- a survey for a course type that doesn't get one automatically, such as an independent work project, an essay, training or a master's thesis (not for exams);
- a course, teacher or student that's still missing from Norppa a few days after the data in Sisu was fixed (Sisu changes normally show the next morning; see [Sisu updates](/guide/course-surveys#sisu-updates));
- an error that doesn't go away.

To help support help you, include a link to the survey's page or the course code, what you tried and what happened.

Ideas and feedback about Norppa itself go to its developers through [[navBar:feedback]] in the top menu.
