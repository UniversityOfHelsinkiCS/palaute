# Anonymity and visibility

All feedback is anonymous. This page has all the rules on who sees which answers, and on how Norppa protects respondents even when there are few of them.

## Answers are anonymous {#anonymous}

- Students log in to Norppa, so it knows who may answer which survey. Teachers and programme staff still can't see who gave which answers.
- Open answers are listed in alphabetical order.
- In downloaded answers, each question's answers are shuffled separately, and there are no timestamps or student information.
- As you can't see who answered what, you can't reward individual students for answering.

## Who sees which answers {#public-answers}

Each question is either [[common:public]] or [[common:notPublic]].

- Responsible teachers, administrative persons and degree programme staff with admin rights see the answers to all questions.
- Teachers, programme staff with read rights and students see only the answers to public questions. Students see them once they have answered themselves, or after the survey has closed.

See [Roles and rights](/guide/roles#course-roles) for who has which role.

By default:

- Scale and choice questions a teacher adds are public.
- Open questions are not public.
- Questions copied from another survey, or carried over from the course's previous realisation, are not public.
- Questions the university or the degree programme has made public are always public, and that can't be changed. These include the university level scale and choice questions.

The answers to a grouping question are never public, and that can't be changed.

A responsible teacher can change whether the other questions are public on their course: besides their own questions, e.g. the university level open question and the programme questions the programme hasn't made public. An open question can be made public only after the survey has closed, as its answers may contain personal data. See [Changing who sees the answers](/guide/results#visibility), and for programme questions, [Making the answers public](/guide/programme-questions#public).

## Courses with fewer than 5 students {#small-courses}

On a small course, a student's answers could be recognised even without a name. So if fewer than 5 students are enrolled, the survey works differently:

- When a student opens the survey, a warning tells them that their answers could be linked to them. They can cancel or continue.
- To submit, the student must tick a box agreeing that the teacher can see their answers even though few students are enrolled.
- Only the course's responsible teachers and administrative persons see the answers. Students, other teachers and programme staff see a note that the results aren't shown.
- The reminder email a week before opening tells the responsible teachers that the course is small.

The limit counts the students enrolled in Norppa, not the answers. It applies to all survey types.

## Answers by group {#groups}

If the course has groups in Sisu, or the survey has a grouping question, the answers can be viewed by group. A single group's answers are shown only if every group has either no answers or at least 5, so that no one can be recognised from them. See [Groups](/guide/questions#groups).

## Who has answered {#who-answered}

Responsible teachers and programme admins see the list of the survey's students. Whether each student has answered is shown only if all of these are true:

- the survey has closed,
- at least 5 students have answered, and
- the course's degree programme allows it in its settings, for all its courses or for this course.

The list never shows who answered what. See [Who has answered](/guide/results#who-answered), and for the programme's setting, [Who sees the respondents](/guide/settings#student-list).

## Data protection {#data-protection}

Norppa is a University of Helsinki service. The [[feedbackView:dataProtectionNotice]] link at the bottom of every Norppa page leads to the university's data protection information, which is collected on the university's [data protection page](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/data-protection).

- Students may write personal data, their own or someone else's, in open answers. Check the answers before making an open question public, and [hide](/guide/results#hide-answers) any that shouldn't be seen.
- In the survey, students can read how their answers are used under the link [[feedbackView:feedbackInfoLink]]

If you have questions about how Norppa handles data, contact [norppa@helsinki.fi](mailto:norppa@helsinki.fi).
