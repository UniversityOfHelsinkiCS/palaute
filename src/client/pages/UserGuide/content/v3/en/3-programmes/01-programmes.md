# The programme's page

Each degree programme and faculty has its own page in Norppa. There, you follow the feedback of its courses. With admin rights, you also add [questions to all its course surveys](/guide/programme-questions), choose its [settings](/guide/settings) and run [programme surveys](/guide/programme-surveys) there.

## Opening the page and its tabs {#programme-page}

Open [[navBar:myProgrammes]] in the top menu (called [[navBar:myOrganisations]] if you also have rights to something other than degree programmes) and choose the programme or faculty. If the menu item is missing, you have no rights to any programme; see [Rights in a degree programme or faculty](/guide/roles#organisation-rights).

The page's tabs depend on your rights. With read rights you see:

- [[organisationSettings:summaryTab]]: the programme's [course summary](/guide/course-summary), with the averages of the programme's own scale questions too.
- [[organisationSettings:courseRealisationsTab]]: the programme's course realisations of the chosen period, with their course and feedback periods, answer counts and responsible teachers. Choose a course to open its survey.
- [[organisationSettings:responsiblesTab]]: the programme's teachers and how many courses each had in the chosen period, downloadable with [[common:exportXLSX]].

Admins, i.e. the programme director, others with admin rights from the IAM groups and the programme's feedback liaisons, also see:

- [[organisationSettings:settingsTab]]: feedback liaisons, who sees the respondents, and which courses collect feedback. See [Programme settings](/guide/settings).
- [[organisationSettings:openQuestionsTab]]: the answers to the open questions of all the programme's courses. See [Open questions](#open-questions).
- [[organisationSettings:surveyTab]] (on a faculty's page [[organisationSettings:surveyFacultyTab]]): questions added to all the programme's course surveys. See [Programme questions](/guide/programme-questions).
- [[organisationSettings:organisationSurveysTab]] (on a faculty's page [[organisationSettings:organisationFacultySurveysTab]]): the programme's own surveys. See [Programme surveys](/guide/programme-surveys).

Admins see all answers of the programme's courses; read rights show only the answers to public questions. See [Who sees which answers](/guide/anonymity#public-answers).

## Open questions {#open-questions}

The [[organisationSettings:openQuestionsTab]] tab (admins only) collects in one place the answers to the university level and programme open questions of all the programme's courses, grouped by course and realisation. The teachers' own questions aren't included.

- Choose [[courseSummary:all]] for every year, or [[courseSummary:filter]] to choose an academic year, semester or period. A realisation is shown if its course dates overlap the chosen time.
- Click a course or realisation to open its summary or its answers.
- Very short answers, under five characters, are left out.
- [[feedbackTargetResults:export]] > [[common:exportPdf]] prints the answers or saves them as a PDF.

## Course surveys as an admin {#course-surveys}

With admin rights, you can also act on your programme's course surveys. Open a survey by choosing the course on the [[organisationSettings:courseRealisationsTab]] tab. You can:

- edit the questions before the survey opens; see [Editing the questions](/guide/questions#edit-questions)
- hide open answers; see [Hiding open answers](/guide/results#hide-answers)
- send reminders; see [Sending a reminder email](/guide/collecting-answers#send-reminder)
- see who has answered; see [Who has answered](/guide/results#who-answered)
- switch on continuous feedback and read it; see [Continuous feedback](/guide/during-the-course#continuous-feedback).

Programme rights don't make you a teacher on a course: to change a course survey's dates or write counter feedback, you need the responsible teacher role in Sisu. Likewise, only responsible teachers can change whether questions are public, create interim feedback and reply to continuous feedback.
