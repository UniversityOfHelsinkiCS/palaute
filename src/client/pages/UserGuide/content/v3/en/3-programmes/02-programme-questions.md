# Programme questions

Programme questions are added to the course surveys of all the course units that belong to the programme in Sisu, after the university level questions. Teachers see them in their survey but can't edit or remove them. The programme's admins add and edit them.

> **Note:** A change shows at once in every course survey of the programme, _also in surveys that are already open or have closed_. Changing a question's wording changes it in surveys that already have answers to it, and removing a question removes it from them. So add programme questions before the semester's surveys open, and add a new question rather than change the meaning of an old one.

## Adding questions {#add}

1. On the programme's page, open the [[organisationSettings:surveyTab]] tab (on a faculty's page [[organisationSettings:surveyFacultyTab]]). The university level questions are at the top; they can't be changed here.
2. Click [[questionEditor:addQuestion]] and choose the question type, or [[questionEditor:addTextualContent]] for a text section.
3. Write the question in Finnish, Swedish and English, and click [[questionEditor:done]].
4. The first time you save, Norppa reminds you that the questions are shared by the whole programme. Confirm.

The questions work like a teacher's own questions; see [Editing the questions](/guide/questions#edit-questions) and [Question types](/guide/questions#question-types).

## Making the answers public {#public}

A new programme question is [[common:notPublic]]: only the course's responsible teachers and the programme's admins see its answers. To show the answers of a scale or choice question to the course's students and teachers as well:

<!-- Remove the reload step once a new programme question's publicity works without it (until a reload, its card shows Public and has no id). -->

1. Reload the page after saving the question.
2. Click the question's [[common:notPublic]] button.
3. Choose [[common:publicInfo]].

When the programme has made a question public, it's public on every course of the programme, and teachers can't change that. Answers to open programme questions can't be made public here; a responsible teacher can make them public on their own course after it has closed. See [Who sees which answers](/guide/anonymity#public-answers).

## Where the questions appear {#placement}

In the survey, students see the programme's scale and choice questions right after the university level ones, and its open questions after the teacher's own questions, before the university level open questions. See [The order students see](/guide/questions#question-order).

The averages of the programme's scale questions are on the programme page's [[organisationSettings:summaryTab]] tab, and the answers to its open questions on the [[organisationSettings:openQuestionsTab]] tab; see [The programme's page](/guide/programmes#programme-page).
