# Continuous and interim feedback

The course survey opens only after the course has ended. To hear from students while the course is still running, a course survey's responsible teachers can use continuous feedback, interim feedback or both. Neither affects the course survey itself, and neither is available in programme surveys.

## Continuous feedback {#continuous-feedback}

With continuous feedback, students can write free-form feedback on the course at any time before the course survey opens. It's off until a responsible teacher or a programme admin turns it on:

1. Go to [[navBar:myCourses]] and choose the course.
2. Open the [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab.
3. Switch on [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedback]].
4. If you want an email about new feedback, switch on [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedbackDigest]].
5. If you like, write a text for the students in [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackPreambleLabel]], e.g. what you'd like feedback on, and click [[common:save]]. It's shown on the feedback form.

[[video:continuous-feedback]]

The switches are saved at once. Norppa doesn't tell the students about it, so let them know. They find the course in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]] on the [[userFeedbacks:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab and write their feedback with [[userFeedbacks:giveContinuousFeedback]].

Students can give continuous feedback until the course survey opens. After that, the feedback they gave stays available.

### Daily email

With [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedbackDigest]] on, the course's responsible teachers get an email at 8:00 each morning with the continuous feedback given in the previous 24 hours. If nothing new came in, no email is sent. Feedback given while the email was off isn't sent later, but you can always read it on the tab.

### Reading and replying

The [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab lists all the feedback under [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackGiven]], newest first. To reply to one:

1. Click [[feedbackTargetView:respondContinuousFeedback]] under it.
2. Write your reply and click [[common:send]].

Only the student who gave the feedback sees your reply. They see it in Norppa and get it by email. You can change the reply later with [[common:edit]], but the changes aren't emailed.

Continuous feedback is anonymous: Norppa doesn't show you who gave it. Responsible teachers and programme admins can read it; teachers without the responsible teacher role can't. It can't be deleted.

While the course is running and continuous feedback is on, [[navBar:myCourses]] shows the [[teacherView:continuousFeedback]] chip on the course.

## Interim feedback {#interim-feedback}

Interim feedback is an extra survey with your own questions, e.g. in the middle of the course. You can create several. Norppa copies the course's students and teachers to it when you create it.

To create one (responsible teachers):

1. Go to [[navBar:myCourses]] and choose the course.
2. Open the [[feedbackTargetView:interimFeedbackTab]] tab.
3. Click [[interimFeedback:addSurvey]].
4. Give it a name in at least one language, and the dates in [[interimFeedback:startDate]] and [[interimFeedback:endDate]].
5. Click [[common:save]]. The new interim feedback opens on its [[feedbackTargetView:surveyQuestionsTab]] tab.
6. Add your questions. See [editing the questions](/guide/questions#edit-questions).

[[video:interim-feedback]]

Interim feedback has no university level or programme questions. If you want the university level questions, add them with [[editFeedbackTarget:copyUniversityQuestions]]. As in course surveys, the questions are locked once it opens.

It opens at 00:00 on its first day and closes at 23:59 on its last day. To change its name or dates, open it with [[interimFeedback:viewFeedback]] and click [[interimFeedback:editSurvey]]. You can do this at any time, e.g. to extend it or to reopen it after it has closed.

### Getting answers

Norppa sends no emails about interim feedback, to students or to teachers. Students see it in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]] while it's open, but they won't know about it unless you tell them. Once it's open, send a reminder email with [[feedbackTargetResults:sendReminder]] on its [[feedbackTargetView:shareTab]] tab, or share its link. See [sharing the survey](/guide/collecting-answers#share-link).

While an interim feedback is open, [[navBar:myCourses]] shows the [[teacherView:interimFeedbackOpen]] chip on the course.

### Results and removing

[[interimFeedback:viewResults]] on the interim feedback opens its answers once there are some. They work as in a course survey: see [results](/guide/results). After it closes, you can write [counter feedback](/guide/counter-feedback) to the students. Interim feedback isn't included in [[navBar:courseSummary]].

To remove an interim feedback, click [[interimFeedback:remove]] on it and confirm. You can remove it only while it has no answers.
