# Visibility of answers

Who sees the answers to a question depends on their role and on whether the question is public.

## Who sees which answers {#public-answers}

Each question is either [[common:public]] or [[common:notPublic]].

- Responsible teachers, administrative persons and degree programme staff with admin rights see the answers to all questions.
- Teachers, programme staff with read rights and students see only the answers to public questions. Students see them once they have answered themselves, or after the survey has closed.

See [roles](/guide/roles#course-roles) for who has which role.

By default:

- Scale and choice questions a teacher adds are public.
- Open questions are not public.
- Questions you copy from another survey, or that are carried over from the course's previous realisation, are not public.
- Questions the university or the degree programme has made public are always public, and you can't change them. These include the university level scale and choice questions.

A responsible teacher can change whether a question is public; see [Changing who sees the answers](/guide/results#change-visibility).

## Courses with fewer than 5 students {#small-courses}

On a small course, a student's answers could be recognised even without a name. So if fewer than 5 students are enrolled, the survey works differently:

- When a student opens the survey, a warning tells them that their answers could be linked to them. They can cancel or continue.
- To submit, the student must tick a box agreeing that the teacher can see their answers even though few students are enrolled.
- Only the course's responsible teachers and administrative persons see the answers. Students, other teachers and programme staff see a note that the results aren't shown.
- The reminder email a week before opening tells the responsible teachers that the course is small.

The limit counts the students enrolled in Norppa, not the answers. It applies to all survey types.

## What students see {#student-view}

- Students see their surveys in Norppa under [[navBar:myFeedbacks]]. Open surveys they haven't answered are under [[userFeedbacks:waitingForFeedbackTab]], with [[userFeedbacks:giveFeedbackButton]].
- After answering, they see a summary of the answers given so far to the public questions. See [Who sees which answers](#public-answers).
- While the survey is open, they can edit their answers or remove them in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]].
- Answering is anonymous. You can't see who answered what, so you can't reward individual students for answering. See [Who has answered](/guide/results#who-answered) for what you can see.

Teachers can't answer their own course's survey: they see it as a [[common:preview]].
