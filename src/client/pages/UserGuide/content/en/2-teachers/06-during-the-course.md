# Continuous and interim feedback

Besides the course survey at the end of the course, teachers can collect feedback during the course in two ways. The responsible teacher can turn on "continuous feedback", an easy, email-based way for students to give quick feedback to the teacher. Teachers can also collect interim feedback with a separate survey, e.g. in the middle of the course. These features are available only in regular course surveys (not in the programmes' own surveys).

## Continuous feedback {#continuous-feedback}

Continuous feedback is given in Norppa, and with it students can write free-form feedback on the course at any time during the course (before the course survey opens). It's off until a responsible teacher or a programme admin (programme director or feedback liaison) turns it on.

To turn on continuous feedback:

1. Go to [[navBar:myCourses]] and choose the course.
2. Open the [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab.
3. Switch on [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedback]].
4. If you want an email about new feedback, switch on [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedbackDigest]].
5. Write a suitable introduction for the students in [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackPreambleLabel]], e.g. what you'd like feedback on, and click [[common:save]]. The introduction is shown on the feedback form.

[[video:continuous-feedback]]

The switches are saved at once, but Norppa doesn't tell students about continuous feedback automatically, so it's a good idea to let them know separately. Students find the course in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]] on the [[userFeedbacks:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab and write their feedback under [[userFeedbacks:giveContinuousFeedback]].

Students can give continuous feedback until the course survey opens. The feedback given is saved in Norppa.

### Daily email

With [[feedbackTargetView:activateContinuousFeedbackDigest]] on, the course's responsible teachers get an email each morning with the continuous feedback given in the previous 24 hours. If nothing new came in, no email is sent. Feedback given while the email was off isn't sent later, but it's saved in Norppa.

### Reading and replying

The [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackTab]] tab lists all the continuous feedback given under [[feedbackTargetView:continuousFeedbackGiven]]. To reply to one:

1. Click [[feedbackTargetView:respondContinuousFeedback]].
2. Write your reply and click [[common:send]].

Only the student who gave the feedback sees the teacher's reply. The student gets the reply by email, and it's also shown in Norppa. You can change the reply later with [[common:edit]], but the changes aren't emailed.

Continuous feedback is anonymous: Norppa doesn't show you who gave it. Responsible teachers and programme admins (programme director and feedback liaison) can read it, but teachers (without the responsible teacher role) can't. It can't be deleted.

While the course is running and continuous feedback is on, [[navBar:myCourses]] shows the [[teacherView:continuousFeedback]] chip on the course.

## Interim feedback {#interim-feedback}

Interim feedback is a separate survey like the course survey, but with your own questions, e.g. in the middle of the course. You can create several. Norppa copies the course's students and teachers to it when you create it.

To create one (responsible teachers):

1. Go to [[navBar:myCourses]] and choose the course.
2. Open the [[feedbackTargetView:interimFeedbackTab]] tab.
3. Click [[interimFeedback:addSurvey]].
4. Give the interim feedback a name in at least one language, and the dates in [[interimFeedback:startDate]] and [[interimFeedback:endDate]].
5. Click [[common:save]]. The new interim feedback opens on its [[feedbackTargetView:surveyQuestionsTab]] tab.
6. Add your questions. See [editing the questions](/guide/questions#edit-questions).

[[video:interim-feedback]]

Interim feedback has no university level or programme questions. If you want the university level questions, add them with [[editFeedbackTarget:copyUniversityQuestions]]. As in course surveys, the questions are locked once it opens.

Interim feedback opens at 00:00 on its opening day and closes at 23:59 on its closing day.

To change its name or dates, open it with [[interimFeedback:viewFeedback]] and click [[interimFeedback:editSurvey]]. You can do this at any time, e.g. to extend it or to reopen it after it has closed.

### Getting answers

Norppa sends no automatic emails about interim feedback, to students or to teachers. Once it's open, send a reminder email with [[feedbackTargetResults:sendReminder]] on its [[feedbackTargetView:shareTab]] tab, or share its link, e.g. by email. See [sharing the survey](/guide/collecting-answers#share-link). Students see the interim feedback in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]].

While an interim feedback is open, [[navBar:myCourses]] shows the [[teacherView:interimFeedbackOpen]] chip on the course.

### Results and removing

[[interimFeedback:viewResults]] on the interim feedback shows the answers collected so far. Technically they work as in a course survey: see [results](/guide/results). After it closes, the responsible teacher can send [counter feedback](/guide/counter-feedback) on it. Interim feedback isn't included in [[navBar:courseSummary]].

To remove an interim feedback, click [[interimFeedback:remove]] and confirm. You can remove it only while it has no answers.
