# Collecting answers

Students get an email when the course survey opens. Teachers can share the survey's link, e.g. at a lecture, and send a reminder through Norppa while the survey is open.

## What emails students receive {#student-emails}

- On a course survey's opening day, the students get an email with a link to the survey and its closing date. Norppa sends it at the daily mailing at 11:15. Students who enrol later get it later, while the survey is open.
- If you open the survey with [[editFeedbackTarget:openImmediately]], the email is sent at once; see [Opening the survey now](/guide/schedule#open-now).
- Interim feedback and programme surveys send students no email by themselves, so the teacher has to share their link and send a reminder separately.

## Sharing the link and QR code {#share-link}

Every survey has a link students use to answer it. To share it:

1. Open the survey from [[navBar:myCourses]].
2. Open the [[feedbackTargetView:shareTab]] tab.
3. Next to [[feedbackTargetView:studentLinkTitle]], click [[common:copyToClipBoard]].

[[video:share-link]]

The tab also has the [[feedbackTargetView:studentLinkQRTitle]], which students can scan with their phones, e.g. from a slide at the last lecture.

The link works before the survey opens too, so you can add it to e.g. Moodle in advance. Students who open it early see when the survey will be open. Only the survey's own students can answer: the students enrolled on the course in Sisu, or the students added as respondents to a programme survey.

All the course's teachers and the programme's admins see the [[feedbackTargetView:shareTab]] tab. On the course page, [[feedbackTargetView:copyLink]] copies the same link. After the survey has closed, the tab also has the [[feedbackTargetView:studentResultsLinkTitle]], see [Counter feedback](/guide/counter-feedback#students-see).

## Sending a reminder email {#send-reminder}

While a survey is open, the teacher can email its students who haven't answered yet. This works the same way in all survey types, and it helps get more answers. To send one:

1. Open the survey's [[feedbackTargetView:shareTab]] tab.
2. Click [[feedbackTargetResults:sendReminder]].
3. Edit the message under [[feedbackTargetResults:writeAMessage]] if you like.
4. Click [[feedbackTargetResults:sendReminderButton]].

[[video:send-reminder]]

The email goes to every student of the survey who hasn't answered yet, and it includes a link to the survey.

- The message is filled in for you in the course's teaching language. If you send it unchanged, each student gets Norppa's standard reminder in their own language. If you edit the message, every student gets your text as you wrote it, so write it in all the languages your students need.
- A reminder can be sent once every 24 hours per survey. After sending, the tab shows when the latest reminder was sent.
- The responsible teachers and administrative persons of the course, the programme's admins (programme director, feedback liaison) and the responsible persons of a programme survey can send reminders.

## What students see {#student-view}

- Students see their surveys in Norppa under [[navBar:myFeedbacks]]. Open surveys they haven't answered are under [[userFeedbacks:waitingForFeedbackTab]], with [[userFeedbacks:giveFeedbackButton]].
- While the survey is open, they can edit their answers or remove them in [[navBar:myFeedbacks]].
- After answering, they see a summary of the answers given so far to the public questions. See [Answer visibility for different users](/guide/anonymity#public-answers).
- If the course is small, i.e. fewer than 5 students are enrolled, students are warned before answering that their answers could be linked to them. See [Courses with fewer than 5 students](/guide/anonymity#small-courses).
- Answering is anonymous, but if at least 5 students have answered, the teacher gets this information and can reward these students, e.g. with an extra point in the exam. See [Who has answered](/guide/results#who-answered) for details.

Teachers can't answer their own course's survey: they see it as a [[common:preview]].
